mission "Atlantean Prologue: Leviathan Signal"
	name "Follow the Leviathan Signal"
	description "An Atlantean operative has pinged you from New Boston, promising fireteam-grade technology if you rendezvous at Earth."
	landing
	priority
	source "New Boston"
	destination "Earth"
	to offer
		not "Atlantean Prologue: Leviathan Signal: offered"
	on offer
		set "Atlantean Prologue: Leviathan Signal: offered"
		conversation
			`A translucent sigil flickers across your wrist-comm the moment you step into the New Boston concourse. The glyph is a stylized trident overlaying a shooter crosshair, rotating until it aligns with your vitals.`
			`			A hooded figure materializes from the crowd. "Vanguard candidate?" she asks, her voice modulated like overlapping choirs. "We've tracked your combat telemetry. Atlantis Sovereignty wants you on the Leviathan protocol."`
			choice
				`			"What is the Leviathan protocol?"`
					goto intel
				`			"I'm not interested."`
					decline
			label intel
			`			"Mythic tech meets tactical precision," she says, handing you a compact datavault. "Fireteams. Roles. Supers. The MMO you wanted to live in, only it's real and it's ours."`
			`			"Dock at Earth. The Citadel choir will sync your neural lattice and slot you into a squad."`
			choice
				`			"I'm in."`
					accept
				`			"Sounds cultish."`
					decline
	on accept
		log "Factions" "Atlantean Sovereignty" `A clandestine human-descended society hidden in Sol's oceans, blending mythic relics with bleeding-edge combat telemetry. They organize their pilots into MMO-like fireteams, using hardlight weapons and living data rituals to dominate close-quarters battles.`
		log "Campaigns" "Leviathan Protocol" `Accepted an invitation to rendezvous with Atlantean handlers on Earth and get briefed on the Leviathan protocol—an MMOFPS-style strike operation promising mythic gear.`

	on complete
		conversation
			`The Atlantica Citadel rises from Earth's Atlantic like a cathedral forged of water and starlight. Inside, choir-beacons sync to your heartbeat while technicians coil luminous cabling around your ship schematic.`
			`			"You don't pilot alone anymore," intones Prefect Naia. "Every strike is a saga. Every headshot a stanza. Take this resonance lance and echo capacitor; they will tune you to the squad's tempo."`
		outfit "Atlantean Resonance Lance"
		outfit "Atlantean Echo Capacitor"
		set "license: Atlantean Vanguard"
		set "Atlantean Rank: Initiate"
		set "Atlantean Level" 1
		set "Atlantean XP" 1
		set "Atlantean Prologue: Leviathan Signal: done"
		event "Atlantean Arsenal Access"
		log "Campaigns" "Leviathan Protocol" `Synced with the Citadel choir on Earth. Received resonance gear and an Atlantean Vanguard license, plus access to an Arsenal outfitter hidden within the capital's hangars.`
